          Cyber Tracker plots newly reported cyberattacks on the globe. Each marker stands where the affected organisation is based, not where the attacker is.

          Everything that gets reported shows up. A solid marker with a white ring and a beam is Confirmed: the organisation itself or a news desk has confirmed the attack. A small hollow ring is Claimed: an attacker listed the victim on a leak site and nobody has verified it yet. Every incident links to where it was reported.

          Markers with a moving ring were reported in the last three days. New incidents appear on the globe while the page is open.

          Controls

          Drag to spin. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Arrow keys and + / − work when the globe has focus. Select a marker or a feed entry to fly to it.
